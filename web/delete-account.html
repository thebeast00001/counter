Deleting your account

Counter keeps your records on your own phone. An account is only needed if you turned on the optional cloud backup — so there are two separate things here, and you can remove either without the other.

From inside the app

  1. Open Settings, then Backup and sign-in.
  2. Scroll to the bottom of the signed-in card.
  3. Tap Delete my account and backup and confirm twice.

This deletes your account and everything stored on the server. It happens immediately — there is no grace period and no way to undo it.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, or the in-app deletion did not complete, email anshtyagi0404@gmail.com from the phone number or address you signed up with, or tell us the phone number on the account. Requests are actioned within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account — the phone number you signed in with, and the sign-in itself.
  • Your cloud backup — every synced record: customers, visits, payments, plans and reminders.

Deleted straight away, and not retained in backups beyond the 30 days our hosting providers take to expire their own copies.

What is not deleted

The records on your phone stay exactly as they are. Deleting an account is not the same as wanting to lose your business, so the app never assumes it. To erase the phone's own copy as well, use Settings → Start over, which wipes everything locally and cannot be undone either.

Counter stores its on-device records encrypted with a key held in your phone's secure hardware. Nobody else — including us — can read them, which also means we cannot delete them for you remotely.
