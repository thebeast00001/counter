Counter

The books, without the bookkeeping

Describe your business in a sentence and Counter builds itself around it — customers, visits, money owed, what needs chasing today. Built for the shops, gyms, salons, tuition centres and studios that run on a notebook and a phone.

Where your records live

On your phone, encrypted with a key held in the phone's own secure hardware. There is no account to create and nothing is uploaded. Cloud backup and the assistant are both optional, both off until you turn them on, and the assistant is never sent a customer's name.
